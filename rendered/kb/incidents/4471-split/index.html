  • parser — the shared date parser reads every date month first. All our tenants write the day first, so bookings land with day and month swapped (“Booked for 2 March. The invite says 3 February”). Fix in the parser: read dates in the tenant’s locale.
  • hint — booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants on booking-v3 (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. A parser fix does not touch this: the hint text and form must follow the locale.
  • one-day — the date moves by one day, not by a swap: “Tuesday 12 May … Monday 11 May”, “Every booking in June is one day off”, “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That is a time zone or daylight-saving bug, not a date-order one. The desk had already noted these reports (desk-notes.json); the #4471 table only footnotes them under parser. They have their own ticket, and they stay counted under parser too, where the desk files them by tenant.

Outcome

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October.

  • parser — fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket closed as completed.
  • hint — won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired. Ticket closed as not planned.
  • one-day — no fix of its own in the update. The desk files these reports under parser, so the ticket is closed with #4471. If one-day moves keep coming after the parser fix, reopen it.

How the counts were made

  • A report’s problem comes from its tenant (locale and booking form) and its channel, as causes.ts does. Intake’s intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and is not used.
  • Counts follow the desk’s own view, as exported (the #4471 table): each report under its tenant’s cause. Reports the desk noted as a one-day move get their own row and ticket, and stay in parser.
  • Every number on this page is computed from the export when the page is built (#lib/kb/problems).
